Real Prometheus and Kubernetes results

Prometheus and promtool 3.5.0 ran locally from the darwin-arm64 archive checked against its published SHA-256. The process queried an isolated synthetic TSDB. No cluster monitoring installation or scrape was used. The TLS gateway used a private ephemeral bearer. Only kind-dev-local-a and kind-dev-local-b were used.

The integration test passed for same-name, distinct-UID Pods and each real control-plane Node. The rerun returned 41 evaluations per Pod metric and 121 per Node metric. Complete five-minute CPU windows returned 200 and 400 mCPU for the app containers and 800 and 1,600 mCPU for root cgroups. The counter reset every eight samples. Working sets were 32, 64, 128 and 256 MiB respectively. The initial test incorrectly expected a full-window rate during series warm-up. The corrected assertion starts after five minutes of counter input. Shared-source cluster selection, absent series and duplicate series were exercised against the real engine. The promtool lookback fixture separately verified zero, a carried 4,096-byte gauge and its disappearance at the five-minute boundary.
