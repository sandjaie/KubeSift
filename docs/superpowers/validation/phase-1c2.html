High-volume native test failed · 2026-11-13

Tested build 3d5cce2 on this Mac using temporary Pod log-volume-uat in kind-dev-local-a / kubesift-explorer. The bounded synthetic source emitted 1,000 lines per second, including UTF-8 text and searchable markers. A ten-second source sample contained 10,000 lines and 2,830,000 timestamped bytes.

The viewer initially showed Live. The subsequent expand/search interaction exceeded the 30-second native automation timeout. WebKit CPU samples reached 98.6–100% of one core and RSS rose from approximately 196 MiB to 379 MiB. Native accessibility inspection still timed out after the source Pod was removed. These observations establish a responsiveness failure in this test; the precise rendering, accessibility or delivery bottleneck remains unisolated.

The temporary Pod was deleted and absence verified. KubeSift was terminated and relaunched; its workspace was accessible again. Search, scrolling, retention-warning visibility and normal tab-leave cleanup under this load remain unverified because the interaction stalled. High-volume readiness is failed, and this supersedes the earlier pending stress-test status. User acceptance remains pending.
