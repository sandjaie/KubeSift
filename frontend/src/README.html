Frontend presentation

App composes the product shell and explicitly entered preview or live validation. ValidationApp retains context/helper/marker controls and the shared register/activate/stop protocol. Its scoped validation.css does not style product controls. bridge.ts owns session listeners and acknowledgements; state.ts retains bounded live histories and explicit session routing. Terminal.tsx owns xterm lifecycle and parse acknowledgements. profile.ts and validation.ts retain the fixture-guarded packaged profile. Product workspace, preview, settings, sessions, UI and AI folders contain presentation only; synthetic identities never enter live service calls.
